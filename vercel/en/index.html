Field guide

Read the wind from three angles

Direction names where the wind comes from

Meteorological wind direction names its source, not its destination. A northerly comes from the north. Read the letter or written direction beside the speed, and check the legend because arrows can differ between products.

Compass degrees start at north: east is 90, south 180 and west 270. Establish that compared products both describe origin. Direction is not a steering instruction or a direct description of wind between nearby buildings.

Why does a forecast show two wind speeds?

A forecast may show general wind and gusts. Gusts are brief increases and can be stronger than the general value. Compare time labels: a maximum for a period differs from a figure at one selected hour.

Ask whether a figure is an average, a value at a selected time, or a maximum gust. Providers define fields differently. Buildings, ridges and open corridors can also change local exposure from the modelled conditions.

Use the same unit before comparing

Metres per second, kilometres per hour and knots express the same quantity. Multiply m/s by 3.6 for km/h; one knot equals 1.852 km/h. For example, 10 m/s is 36 km/h or approximately 19.4 knots.

Keep units beside results and round sensibly. Twenty knots does not equal twenty kilometres per hour. Changing the unit neither updates the forecast nor improves its confidence: it changes how an existing speed is expressed.

Place wind inside the full weather picture

Compare wind with gusts, rain chance and temperature during the hours you will be outside. Consider whether your route or destination is open or sheltered. A wind number supports a decision; it cannot automatically approve an activity.

Start with place, date and update time when comparing Saudi cities. Do not substitute coastal conditions for a mountain location. Marine, aviation and warning-related decisions require the relevant specialist forecasts and official instructions.

Questions that make forecasts clearer

Does a northerly wind travel towards the north?

In weather terminology it comes from the north and generally moves southwards. An arrow may instead show the direction of movement. Read the written compass direction or the product legend as well as the graphic before comparing two forecasts.

Will a forecast gust last for the entire hour?

No. A gust describes a brief increase, not a steady speed throughout the interval. Check whether the provider shows the expected maximum gust or another definition, and read it beside the general wind for the same time period.

How do I convert knots to kilometres per hour?

Multiply knots by 1.852. For example, 10 knots is 18.52 km/h. This is arithmetic rather than an assessment of weather risk: suitability for an activity also depends on context, gusts, other conditions and any official warnings.

Why does the wind outside differ from the forecast?

A model represents an area rather than your exact street corner. Obstacles, terrain and height influence local exposure. Check location, time and the provider's description; a short-lived difference alone does not prove that another source measures conditions beside you.
