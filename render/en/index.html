Three ways to read rain, one habit of checking

Probability is not a fraction of your day

When a product gives a precipitation probability for a location and period, it describes the chance of the defined event. A 40% value does not automatically mean rain for 40% of your appointment, and it is not a rainfall quantity. Read the variable's label and time window first.

Providers can use different measurable-rain thresholds and forecast intervals. Do not transfer one service's precise definition automatically to every app. To compare weather today and tomorrow, choose equivalent periods: a one-hour probability and a whole-day probability answer different questions even when their percentages happen to look similar.

Amount and duration answer another question

Rainfall amount is commonly expressed in millimetres as a depth over a level area. One millimetre corresponds to one litre per square metre before infiltration, runoff and evaporation are considered. This unit relationship cannot predict the depth of water in a street or determine whether a road is passable.

Read the timing beside the total. Rain spread over several hours differs from an amount concentrated into a shorter interval. Without an hourly breakdown, do not infer shower intensity from the total alone. Do not add hourly percentages to calculate a daily probability, or assume that every displayed accumulation is an additional independent amount.

Thunder is more than a larger rain total

Lightning and thunder introduce a hazard that rainfall quantity alone cannot describe. Storm hazards can occur outside the heaviest rain, so a low precipitation probability at one point is not a reason to disregard a developing nearby storm. Follow official information for your actual location, not only the destination you originally planned.

If you hear thunder outdoors, move into a suitable enclosed building or a metal-topped vehicle with its windows closed, and follow local official instructions. An umbrella or open-sided picnic shelter does not provide equivalent protection. This page does not declare a day storm-free from a general icon or missing forecast data.

Turn a forecast into a plan you can revisit

Before a rain-sensitive activity, define its time window, an alternative location and a way to return. Compare adjacent hours rather than expecting one number to decide for you. When reading weather today in Jeddah, Riyadh or Abha, remember that showers need not be distributed evenly across surrounding neighbourhoods.

For Saudi warnings, check the governorate, stated phenomenon, start time and expiry on the National Center for Meteorology service. A warning link does not establish that an alert is active. Equally, the absence of an alert on this page does not establish that none exists officially. Observations, forecasts and warnings are different products with their own sources and updates.

Questions that make forecasts clearer

Does a 60% rain chance mean heavy rain?

No. Probability alone does not establish intensity. Read the expected amount, forecast interval and relevant warning details.

Can I add the hourly rain probabilities together?

No. Adding percentages does not give a daily probability. Use a dedicated daily product or interpret each window separately.

Why can a nearby neighbourhood be wet while mine is dry?

Local showers can vary over short distances. Model grids do not guarantee identical rainfall across every neighbourhood.

Does a low rain chance rule out a thunderstorm hazard?

No single percentage establishes that. Review storm information and warnings, and respond to thunder at your actual location.
